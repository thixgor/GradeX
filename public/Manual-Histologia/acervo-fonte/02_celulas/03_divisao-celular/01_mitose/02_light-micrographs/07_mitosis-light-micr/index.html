Anaphase

These three panels show progressive stages of anaphase. The left panel shows early anaphase in which the chromosomes are beginning to migrate apart. This migration, facilitated by the microtubules of the spindle apparatus, continues (middle and right panels), as the chromosomes become segregated to opposite poles of the cell. Esophageal epithelium, 1000x
