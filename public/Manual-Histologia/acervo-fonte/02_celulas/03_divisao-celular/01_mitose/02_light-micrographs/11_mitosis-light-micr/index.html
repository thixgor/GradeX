Late telophase and cytokinesis

These images show two cells in the lining epithelium of the esophagus in the final stages of cytokinesis and telophase. In the left panel, the daughter cells are still attached by a thin connection, whereas in the right panel, the daughter cells are completely separate. The chromosomes are decondensing and the nuclear envelopes are reforming. Esophageal epithelium 1000x
