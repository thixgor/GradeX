Late telophase and cytokinesis

An electron micrograph shows two daughter cells in late telophase and cytokinesis. The chromosomes are still condensed and the nuclear envelopes have not started to reform. In the cell on the left, microtubules are seen extending from the centriole to the chromosomes. Inset: Magnified view of the area in the box. 5000x; Inset 15,000x
