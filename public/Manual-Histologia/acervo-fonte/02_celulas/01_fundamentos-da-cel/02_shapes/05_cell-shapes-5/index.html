Cell shapes: squamous

In the lining epithelium of the esophagus, squamous cells are present in multiple layers. This arrangement allows them to resist abrasion by sloughing off without damage to the underlying tissue. Note how the rounded basal cells become flattened as they reach the surface and how the shape of the nucleus conforms to the shape of each cell. 1000x
