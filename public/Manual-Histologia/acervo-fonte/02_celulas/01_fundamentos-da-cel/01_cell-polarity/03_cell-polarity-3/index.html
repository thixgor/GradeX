Polarity

These cells in the pituitary gland are examples of non-polarized cells. Note how the secretory granules are homogenously distributed throughout the cytoplasm and not restricted to one region of the cell. This arrangement reflects the fact that these cells can release their secretory product from any site on the cell surface. Pituitary gland, 1000x.
