Rough endoplasmic reticulum

This high magnification electron micrograph shows ribosomes aligned along the cytoplasmic surface of the membranes of the endoplasmic reticulum. Ribosomes are approximately 25 nanometers in diameter and synthesize proteins, which are either inserted into the RER membrane or are translocated into the lumen of the cistern. 65,000x
