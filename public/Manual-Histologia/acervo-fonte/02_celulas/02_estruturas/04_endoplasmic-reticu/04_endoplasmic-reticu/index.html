Rough endoplasmic reticulum

RER

RER consists of a system of interconnected, flattened, membranous sacs or cisterns which have ribosomes on their outer (cytoplasmic) surfaces. In this image, RER fills most of the cytoplasm. Secretory proteins synthesized by ribosomes enter the lumen of the cistern and are transported to the Golgi.
