Rough endoplasmic reticulum

The RER consists of flattened, interconnected membranous sacs (cisterns) continuous with the nuclear envelope. RER possesses ribosomes on the cytoplasmic surface of the membrane. Proteins synthesized by the RER either accumulate within the cisterns or inserted into the membrane during translation. They are transported, via transport vesicles, to the Golgi for processing and packaging. 58,000x
