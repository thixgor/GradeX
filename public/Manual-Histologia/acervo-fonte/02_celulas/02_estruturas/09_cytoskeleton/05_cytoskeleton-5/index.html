Intermediate filaments

In addition to providing intracellular support, intermediate filaments also stabilize adherent junctions between cells and attachment junctions between cells and the extracellular matrix. The cell-to-matrix attachments are called hemidesmosomes (left); the cell-to-cell attachments are called desmosomes (right). 30,000x, 70,000x
