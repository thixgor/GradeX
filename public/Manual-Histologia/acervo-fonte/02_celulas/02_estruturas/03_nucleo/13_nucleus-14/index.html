Nuclear pores

The nuclear envelope contains openings called nuclear pores, which consist of a complex of proteins encircling an opening. Nuclear pores regulate the bi-directional transit of materials between the cytoplasm and nucleus. In this image, six pores are visible and are marked by sites where the perinuclear space is obscured and the marginal/perinuclear heterochromatin is interrupted. 38,000x
