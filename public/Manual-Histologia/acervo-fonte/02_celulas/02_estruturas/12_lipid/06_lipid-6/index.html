Lipid droplets

This electron micrograph shows a cell whose cytoplasm contains numerous lipid droplets. Lipid droplets are bounded by a single layer of phospholipids, not a unit membrane. The monolayer is coated with proteins that provide structural integrity, regulate lipid metabolism and facilitate droplet docking with other organelles. Lipids droplets often coalesce with each other. 6000x
