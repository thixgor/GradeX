Golgi apparatus

An electron micrograph of an active fibroblast shows a prominent Golgi apparatus with its associated vesicles. This cell also shows large accumulations of RER, a feature consistent with a prominent Golgi apparatus. Newly synthesized proteins in the RER are transported to the Golgi by way of transport vesicles. 12,000x
