Clusters of endocrine cells

These images show two special histochemical stains used to distinguish among the different cell types in the islet of Langerhans. The aldehyde fuchsin method (left) stains insulin-secreting beta cells blue. The Gomori’s method (right) stains insulin-secreting beta cells light blue and glucagon-secreting alpha cells pink. Beta cells comprise about 70% of the islet cells, alpha cells about 20%. The remaining somtatostatin-secreting delta cells are not distinguished by these methods. 400x, 400x
