Adrenal cortex

The heavily vacuolated cells of zona fasciculata are arranged in uniform rows (fascicles), which alternate with wide-diameter, fenestrated capillaries. The cells of zona reticularis form an interlacing network, are smaller and stain more intensely than cells in the zona fasciculata. Cells of zona reticularis are also surrounded by fenestrated capillaries. 400x
