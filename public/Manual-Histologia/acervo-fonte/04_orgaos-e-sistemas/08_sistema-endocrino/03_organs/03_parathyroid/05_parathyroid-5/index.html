Parathyroid gland

The special stain used in these images clearly reveals the distribution of oxyphil cells among the more numerous principal cells. Principal cells have sparse amounts of cytoplasm and heterochromatic nuclei. Oxyphil cells have more abundant cytoplasm, which stains reddish due to presence of large numbers of mitochondria. 400x, 1000x
