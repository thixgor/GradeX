Epididymis

A longitudinal section of the epididymis reveals its comma shape. The epididymis is divided into a head region, a body and a tail. The head contains both the efferent ducts coiled into cone shapes, as well as the duct of the epididymis; the body and tail both contain only the single duct of the epididymis. The section shown here consists only of the head and body of the epididymis. 10x

Coni vasculosi

Efferent ducts, located in the head region, are coiled into cone shapes called coni vasculosi. Coni are surrounded by connective tissue and abundant blood vessels, hence their name. Efferent ducts anastomose to form the single duct of the epididymis found in all three subdivisions of the epididymis.
