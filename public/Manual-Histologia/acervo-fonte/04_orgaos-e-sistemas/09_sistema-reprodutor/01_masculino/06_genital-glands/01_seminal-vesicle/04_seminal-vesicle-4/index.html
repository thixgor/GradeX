Seminal vesicle

The central lumen of the seminal vesicle has been partitioned by primary, secondary and tertiary arches composed of the lining epithelium and its underlying lamina propria. The presence of a central lumen and the numerous, intersecting arches surrounding it help to distinguish the seminal vesicle from the ampulla of the oviduct. At the periphery of the gland is a layer of smooth muscle. 100x
