Seminal vesicle

These two images of the seminal vesicle show the spaces formed by the arching of the epithelium and its underlying lamina propria. Pseudostratified epithelium in nonsecretory stages (left) is lower than that seen during active secretion (right). Smooth muscle surrounds the periphery of the gland. 200x, 400x
