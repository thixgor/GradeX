Spermatic cord

The ductus deferens is lined with pseudostratified epithelium overlying a thin lamina propria. The most diagnostic feature, however, is its thick muscular coat of inner and outer tunics of longitudinally oriented smooth muscle and a middle coat of circular muscle. The differentiation between the green connective tissue and red smooth muscle is readily obvious with this trichrome stain. 40x
