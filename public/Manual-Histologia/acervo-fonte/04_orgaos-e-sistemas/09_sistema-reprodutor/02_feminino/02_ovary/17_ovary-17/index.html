Ovary: corpus albicans

A corpus albicans is the scar tissue remains of the corpus luteum; its size decreases with age. The corpus albicans is composed of inactive fibroblasts and scar tissue, which gives the corpus alibcans its distinctive white appearance in fresh tissue and eosinophilic staining in typical slide preparations. 100x
