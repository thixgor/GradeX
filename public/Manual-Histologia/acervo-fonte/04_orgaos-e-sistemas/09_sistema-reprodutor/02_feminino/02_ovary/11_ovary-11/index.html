Ovary: atretic follicles

Atretic follicles are the degenerating remains of follicles. Approximately two million primordial follicles, containing primary oocytes, are present at birth, but only 400-450 secondary oocytes are ovulated from Graafian follicles during the reproductive life of a woman; the remaining follicles undergo atresia. The process of atresia can occur at all stages of follicular development. 100x
