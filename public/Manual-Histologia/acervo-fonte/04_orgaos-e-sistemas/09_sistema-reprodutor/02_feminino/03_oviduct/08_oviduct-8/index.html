Oviduct: intramural portion

The intramural oviduct lies within the uterine wall, surrounded by the uterine musculature. In the intramural oviduct, the folds of the mucosa are reduced to just a few ridges; secretory cells outnumber ciliated cells, and the muscularis externa is thick. 40x
