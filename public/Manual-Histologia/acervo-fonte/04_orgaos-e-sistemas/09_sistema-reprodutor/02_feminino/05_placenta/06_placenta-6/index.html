Placenta: 3rd trimester

Villi appear similar to those in the early placenta except that fetal connective tissue is more condensed; cytotrophoblast cells form a discontinuous layer beneath the syncytiotrophoblast; fibrinoid is seen in villi; and syncytiotrophoblastic nuclei tend to clump. Fetal capillaries are more numerous and are frequently located immediately beneath the syncytial layer. 400x
