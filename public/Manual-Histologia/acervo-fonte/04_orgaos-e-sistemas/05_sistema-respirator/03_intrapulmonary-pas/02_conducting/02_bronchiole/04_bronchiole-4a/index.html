Bronchiole

This higher magnification images shows the components of the bronchiole wall. This larger bronchiole is lined by a pseudostratified columnar epithelium with cilia and goblet cells. Abundant, longitudinally arranged elastic fibers are located beneath the epithelium. The smooth muscle layer is composed of spiraling bands of muscle fibers. 600x

Bronchial vessels

These capillaries are part of the bronchial blood supply that provides oxygen and nutrients to the wall of respiratory passageways. They are not involved in gas exchange with the air.
