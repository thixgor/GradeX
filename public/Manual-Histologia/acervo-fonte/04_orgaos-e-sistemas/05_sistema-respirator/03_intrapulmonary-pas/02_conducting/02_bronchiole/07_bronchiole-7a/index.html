Terminal bronchiole

Club cells first appear in terminal bronchioles and continue into respiratory bronchioles. They are not ciliated and their apical ends bulge into the lumen of the airway. Club cells protect the epithelium by degrading inhaled toxins and their lipoprotein secretion prevents luminal adhesion. Club cells also serve as progenitors for themselves and ciliated cells. 600x
