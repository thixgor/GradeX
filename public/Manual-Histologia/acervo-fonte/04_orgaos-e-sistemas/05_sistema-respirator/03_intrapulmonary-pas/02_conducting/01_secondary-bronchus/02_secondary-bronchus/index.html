Bronchus

A higher magnification image shows the details of the bronchial wall. The mucosa consists of a pseudostratified columnar epithelium with cilia and goblet cells, a lamina propria rich in elastic fibers and a prominent circularly-arranged smooth muscle layer. The submucosa contains mixed, sero-mucous glands and larger bronchial blood vessels. Cartilage plates are present in the outer fibrocartilagenous layer. 200x
