Interalveolar septum

This electron micrograph shows many of the components of the interalveolar septum illustrated in the previous illustration. Particularly thin type I cells line the entire alveolus and the bulging of capillaries into the air space is prominent. Surfactant is not preserved during fixation of lung tissue, and no septal cells nor macrophages are present in this image. 4000x
