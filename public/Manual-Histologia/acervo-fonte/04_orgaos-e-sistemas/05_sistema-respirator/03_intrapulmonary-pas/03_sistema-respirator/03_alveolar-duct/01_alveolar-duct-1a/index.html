Transition of respiratory bronchiole to alveolar duct

Respiratory bronchioles transition to alveolar ducts, elongated passageways whose walls consist almost entirely of alveoli. The opening of each alveolus is surrounded by a ring of smooth muscle that appears like a knob in cross section. Each respiratory bronchiole branches to form multiple alveolar ducts that end in alveolar sacs. 40X
