Bronchial blood vessels

A bronchial vein is seen in the submucosa of a smaller bronchus. Like other systemic veins, bronchial veins carry deoxygenated blood. The majority of bronchial blood drains into pulmonary veins; the remainder enters the systemic venous system. 200x
