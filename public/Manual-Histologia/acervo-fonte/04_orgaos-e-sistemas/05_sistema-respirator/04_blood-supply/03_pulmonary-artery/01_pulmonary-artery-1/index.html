Bronchial vessels

Bronchial vessels are systemic blood vessels that lie within the tissues that form the wall of respiratory passageways. Bronchial arteries supply oxygen and nutrients to the tissues; bronchial veins drain deoxygenated blood away. Bronchial arteries originate as branches of the descending thoracic aorta; bronchial veins drain mostly into pulmonary veins with the remainder entering the systemic venous system.
