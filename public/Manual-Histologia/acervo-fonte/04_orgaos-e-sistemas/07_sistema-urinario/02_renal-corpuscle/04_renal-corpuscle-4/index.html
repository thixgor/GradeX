Podocytes

The visceral layer of Bowman’s capsule is composed of specialized cells called podocytes, which possess numerous foot processes that wrap around the glomerular capillaries. These cells, along with the fenestrated endothelium of the glomerular capillaries and their fused basal laminae, form the glomerular filtration barrier.

Proximal convoluted tubule

The proximal convoluted tubule is lined by a tall cuboidal epithelium with a brush border. The numerous vacuole-appearing structures in the cytoplasm are artifactually-disrupted mitochondria. This distal convoluted tubule shows the characteristic low cuboidal epithelial lining with irregularly spaced nuclei.
