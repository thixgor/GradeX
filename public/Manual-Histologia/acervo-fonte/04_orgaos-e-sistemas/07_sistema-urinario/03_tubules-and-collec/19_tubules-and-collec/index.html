Renal papilla

The apex of the pyramid projects into the minor calyx as the renal papilla. The openings of the papillary ducts of Bellini empty into the lumen of the minor calyx, forming the area cribrosa. The minor calyx is lined by a transitional epithelium, which may be modified as it is reflected over the surface of the papilla. 200x
