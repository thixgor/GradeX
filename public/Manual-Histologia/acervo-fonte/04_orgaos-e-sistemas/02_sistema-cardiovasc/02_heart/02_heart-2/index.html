Endocardium

The endocardium lines the heart chambers and is continuous with the tunica intima of blood vessels; it is thicker in the atria than in the ventricles. The endocardium consists of an endothelium (simple squamous epithelium), an underlying layer of subendothelial connective tissue that may also contain smooth muscle fibers, and a deeper layer of subendocardial connective tissue. Elements of the conducting system of the heart, Purkinje fibers, are located in the subendocardial connective tissue layer. 10x, 400x, 400x
