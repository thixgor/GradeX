Atrioventricular bundle

The atrioventricular (AV) bundle, part of the conducting system, is in septum membranaceum just above the muscular part of the septum. Right and left bundle branches begin in the AV bundle and conduct impulses to the right and left ventricles, respectively. Fibers in these three subdivisions are modified cardiac muscle fibers that are usually smaller than normal cardiac fibers.
