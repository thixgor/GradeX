Myocardium

Diagnostic features of cardiac muscle fibers are branching fibers, intercalated discs and centrally-located nuclei. Intercalated discs, appearing as thick lines, are specialized junctions between fibers; each is composed of interdigitating plasma membranes (sarcolemmae), myofilaments and cell junctions. 400x
