Fenestrated capillaries

The endothelium of this fenestrated capillary possesses clusters of small pores or fenestrations. Fenestrations are not open, rather, they are spanned by diaphragms derived from the glycocalyx. Diaphragms are lacking in the fenestrated capillaries of the renal glomerulus. 9000x
