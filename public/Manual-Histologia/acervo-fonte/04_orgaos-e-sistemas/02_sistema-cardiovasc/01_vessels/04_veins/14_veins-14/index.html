Large vein (vena cava)

At higher magnification, the components of the three tunics are evident in this H & E stained cross section. The tunica intima contains smooth muscle in the subendothelial connective tissue. The tunica media consists of circularly-arranged muscle. The tunica adventitia contains large amounts of longitudinally-oriented smooth muscle fibers interspersed with connective tissue. 400x
