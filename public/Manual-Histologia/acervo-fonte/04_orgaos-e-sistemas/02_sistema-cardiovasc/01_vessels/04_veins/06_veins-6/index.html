High endothelial venules

A unique venule, the high endothelial venule (HEV), is located in some components of the lymphoid system. HEVs are lined by endothelial cells that are cuboidal, rather than squamous, which allow for migration of white blood cells into the surrounding tissue spaces (diapedesis). 600x, 800x
