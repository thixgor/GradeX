Large vein (vena cava)

Because this large vein is sectioned longitudinally, the orientation of the smooth muscle fibers is reversed from that shown in the previous cross sections. In this orientation, longitudinally-oriented smooth muscle fibers in tunicae intima and adventitia are seen in longitudinal section, and the circularly arranged smooth muscle in the tunica media is seen in cross section. This section was stained for elastic fibers, thus an internal elastic lamina, which is present in larger veins, is prominent. 100x, 250x
