Medium artery and vein

Arteries and veins of similar caliber often travel together along with nerves. They are surrounded by a connective tissue sheath forming a neurovascular bundle. In this image, compare the relative thicknesses of the tunica media (thicker in artery) and tunica adventitia (thicker in vein) in these medium-sized vessels. A valve is present in the vein and represents a modification of the tunica intima. These valves prevent the backflow of blood due to gravity. Valves are not present in arteries. 40x
