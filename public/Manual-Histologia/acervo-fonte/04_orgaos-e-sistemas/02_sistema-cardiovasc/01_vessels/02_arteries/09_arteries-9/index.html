Transitional artery

The transition from an elastic artery to a muscular one is gradual, so transitional arteries may show features of both arterial types. This section, stained with a trichrome stain, shows an artery in which the tunica media has characteristics of a medium, muscular artery (inner portion of smooth muscle) and a large, elastic artery (outer portion with elastic lamellae). 100x

Tunica media

The tunica media of this transitional artery has characteristics of both medium, muscular and large, elastic arteries. The inner portion shows circularly arranged smooth muscle cells (red) like a muscular artery, while the outer portion contains elastic lamellae (pink) like an elastic artery.
