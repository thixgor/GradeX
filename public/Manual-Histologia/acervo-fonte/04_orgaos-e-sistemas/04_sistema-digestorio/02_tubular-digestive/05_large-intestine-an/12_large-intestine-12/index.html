Recto-anal junction

The epithelium changes abruptly at the junction of the rectum (simple columnar) with the anal canal (stratified squamous moist). The rectum resembles the rest of the large intestine, having straight intestinal glands with many goblet cells. A more gradual transition of the epithelium occurs between the anal canal and the anus, the latter being lined by a stratified squamous, keratinized epithelium. 100x
