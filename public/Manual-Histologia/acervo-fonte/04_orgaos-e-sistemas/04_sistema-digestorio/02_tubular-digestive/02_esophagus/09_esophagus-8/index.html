Esophagus: Muscularis externa

In the middle part of the muscularis externa of the esophagus, smooth muscle fibers and skeletal muscle fibers occur together. In this field, compare the size of the fibers and location of the nuclei of these two muscle types. Striations are also evident in the longitudinally-sectioned skeletal muscle fibers. 1000x
