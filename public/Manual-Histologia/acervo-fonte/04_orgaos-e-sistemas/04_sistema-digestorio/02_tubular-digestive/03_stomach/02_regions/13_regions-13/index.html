Gastro-duodenal junction

At the gastro-duodenal junction, the mucus-secreting, simple columnar epithelium (sheet gland) of the pylorus changes to a simple columnar epithelium of absorptive cells (enterocytes) and goblet cells in the duodenum. Additionally, the duodenum can be differentiated by its villi and by submucosal gland (Brunner’s glands) in the submucosa. 100x
