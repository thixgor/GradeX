Stomach: pylorus

A trichrome stain demonstrates the pyloric mucosa. Deep, wide gastric pits, interconnecting the surface with the pyloric glands, are longer than the glands. Pyloric glands possess mostly mucus-secreting cells and are quite tortuous, so that they are cut in multiple planes when sectioned. 100x
