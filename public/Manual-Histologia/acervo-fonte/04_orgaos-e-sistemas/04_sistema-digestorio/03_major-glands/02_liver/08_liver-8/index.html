Liver: sinusoids

Sinusoids, discontinuous capillaries, are lined by a fenestrated endothelium with additional gaps between adjacent cells. The discontinuous basal lamina allows easy access between the sinusoid and the space of Disse, located between sinusoids and adjacent hepatocytes. Kupffer cells (liver macrophages) span sinusoids, phagocytosing aged erythrocytes and debris. 5000x
