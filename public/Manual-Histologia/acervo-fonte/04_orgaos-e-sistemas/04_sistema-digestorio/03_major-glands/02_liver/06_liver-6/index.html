Liver: central vein

A central vein is located at the center of a classic liver lobule. The central vein receives blood from the hepatic sinusoids separating plates of hepatocytes. These plates form anastomosing rows that radiate out from the central vein. Central veins anastomose to form three hepatic veins that drain into the inferior vena cava. 800x
