Lip: Vermilion zone

The vermilion zone is lined by a thick keratinized, stratified squamous epithelium and hair follicles are lacking. The numerous, densely packed dermal papillae of the lamina propria allow blood vessels close access to the surface, imparting a red color to this zone. The image on the right shows these details at higher magnification 100x, 400x
