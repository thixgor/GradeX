Tongue: Filiform and fungiform papillae

Filiform and fungiform papillae. Filiform papillae with their conical morphology surround a fungiform papilla, which protrudes slightly above the filiform. Both types of papillae are covered by a keratinized epithelium. Although scattered taste buds may be present on the dorsum of fungiform papillae, none are visible here. 100x
