Oral mucosa: Lining

Lining mucosa lines all of the oral cavity except the gingiva, hard palate, and the dorsum of the tongue. It is composed of stratified squamous moist epithelium and its underlying lamina propria; a muscularis mucosae is lacking. A submucosa lies beneath the mucosa and usually contains the minor salivary glands (mixed glands). 100x
