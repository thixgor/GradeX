Tooth

A section of ground tooth shows the dentin and the dentinal tubules. Dentin is the most similar to bone of all the hard tissues of the tooth. The dentinal tubules, housing odontoblast processes, form S-shaped curves and extend from the pulp chamber, where the odontoblasts are located, to the dentino-enamel or the dentino-cementum junctions. 200x
