Semicircular canals

Three membranous semicircular ducts are shown. One is visible at its attachment with the utricle, just beginning to enter its semicircular canal. The second is sectioned at a distance from the utricle, lying in its bony semicircular canal. The third semicircular duct is represented by its ampulla and the enclosed crista ampullaris. 30x
