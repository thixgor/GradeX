Left panel

Middle panel

Right panel

The Eustachian tube opening is lined by a ciliated columnar epithelium (blue arrows), which becomes pseudostratified columnar with cilia and goblet cells closer to the junction of the Eustachian tube with the nasopharynx. Cilia of this epithelium beat toward the nasopharynx. Supporting cartilage is indicated by a red arrow.
