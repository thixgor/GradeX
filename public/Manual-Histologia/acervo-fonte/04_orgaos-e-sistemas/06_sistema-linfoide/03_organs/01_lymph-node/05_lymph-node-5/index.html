Lymph node: cortex

The outer cortex of a lymph node is filled with B-dependent lymphoid nodules and lies just beneath the capsule and the subcapsular sinus. A portion of two trabecular sinuses are also present, but these sinuses are difficult to appreciate as they are filled with cells. 100x
