Lymph vessels

Diffuse lymphoid tissue, as well as solitary and aggregated lymphoid nodules in the lamina propria, filter tissue fluid that eventually passes into lymphatic capillaries as lymph. Lymph capillaries are the blind-ended beginnings of a complex network of vessels that eventually drain through lymph nodes and into the blood vascular system. Lymph contains high levels of protein and thus the lumen of lymphatic vessels often stains pink with eosin. Esophagus 200x
