Arrector pili muscle

The arrector pili muscle is band of smooth muscle anchored at one end in the papillary layer of the dermis and attached to the hair follicle at the other, in a specialized stem-cell niche called the follicular bulge. Contraction of the arrector pili elevates the hair shaft and produces depressions on the skin surface called “goose flesh”.
