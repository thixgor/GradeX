Smooth muscle

The cytoplasm of smooth muscle fibers cut in cross section appears homogeneous since myofibrils are not present. Actin and myosin myofilaments criss-cross throughout the cytoplasm. In addition, since the nucleus is located midway along the fiber, most cross sections of the fibers do not pass through the nucleus 1000x
