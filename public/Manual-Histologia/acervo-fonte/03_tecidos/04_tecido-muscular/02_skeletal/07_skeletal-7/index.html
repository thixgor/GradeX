Skeletal muscle

This H&E section shows muscle fibers cut in cross section and clearly demonstrates the myofibrils that fill the muscle fibers. Numerous capillaries are also visible. This is a section of the tongue which contains interlacing skeletal muscle fascicles and thus at the top and bottom of the image are fibers cut longitudinally. 800x
