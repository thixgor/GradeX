Muscle insertions

Two muscles (arrows) insert into this diaphysis. Because the force exerted by a muscle is unidirectional, tendon and the fibrocartilage continuing from it contain collagen fiber bundles arranged in a parallel manner. This pattern, therefore, resembles dense regular connective tissue. The region outlined by the rectangle indicates the transition of tendon to fibrocartilage.
