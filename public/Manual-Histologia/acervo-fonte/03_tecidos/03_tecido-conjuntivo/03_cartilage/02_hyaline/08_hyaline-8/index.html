Hyaline cartilage

Chondrocytes lie in potential spaces in cartilage called lacunae. Shrinkage of chondrocytes during tissue preparation creates the impression of a true space surrounding these cells. In life, this space is filled with matrix. Nutrients must diffuse through the cartilage matrix to reach the chondrocytes, so chondrocytes store glycogen and lipid as energy sources.
