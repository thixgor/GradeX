Endochondral ossification

Endochondral ossification begins in a hyaline cartilage model of the future bone. Cartilage located in the center of the cartilage model undergoes a series of regressive, degenerative changes, forming a framework on which bone can be deposited. Because bone is deposited on a cartilage framework, this bone formation is called endochondral ossification. In addition, a sleeve of bone, the periosteal collar, is deposited by intramembranous ossification around the central cartilage shaft by the original perichondrium that differentiates into a periosteum.
