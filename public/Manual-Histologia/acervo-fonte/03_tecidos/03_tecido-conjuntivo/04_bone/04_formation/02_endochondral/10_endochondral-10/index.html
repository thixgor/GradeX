Zone of ossification

In the zone of ossification, osteoblasts deposit woven bone (blue arrows), on the calcified cartilage framework. The distinctly red-staining bone is readily distinguishable from the blue-staining calcified cartilage matrix. Because this bone is replacing cartilage, this type of bone formation called endochondral bone formation.
