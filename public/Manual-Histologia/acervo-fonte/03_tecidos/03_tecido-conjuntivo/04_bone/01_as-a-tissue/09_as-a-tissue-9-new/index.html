Mature bone

The matrix of mature bone is organized in layers or lamellae with osteocytes located in orderly arranged lacunae between the lamellae. This bone is also referred to as lamellar bone.

Immature bone

Immature bone is usually present only temporarily and is replaced by lamellar bone through the process of remodeling. The matrix of immature bone lacks the orderly arrangement of lamellae and the osteocytes appear randomly arranged. This bone is also referred to as woven bone.
