Loose connective tissue

Loose connective tissue consists of a variety of numerous cells, abundant, gelatinous ground substance, and small caliber collagen fibers mixed with elastic and reticular fibers. Elastic and reticular fibers are better distinguished with special stains. The nuclei of active and inactive fibroblasts, adipose cells and macrophages are visible in this image. 400x
