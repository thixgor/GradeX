Loose connective tissue

The dermis of the skin is composed of loose connective tissue interfacing with an underlying dense irregular connective tissue. The loose connective tissue is more cellular, with smaller caliber collagen and elastic fibers than the coarser, dense irregular connective tissue. This toluidine blue-stained section shows the elastic fibers to advantage. 1000x
