Active fibroblast

Active fibroblasts are resident cells found in connective tissues. Their nuclei are euchromatic and elongated. This toluidine blue stain of loose connective tissue beneath an epithelium demonstrates the pale-staining collagen fibers and heavily stained elastic fibers. Blood vessels are also visible. 1000x
