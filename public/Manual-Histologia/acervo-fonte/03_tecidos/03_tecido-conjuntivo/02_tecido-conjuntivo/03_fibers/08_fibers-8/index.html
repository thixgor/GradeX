Elastic and collagen fibers

This section of skin is stained with the Movat pentachrome stain. This combination stain distinguishes elastic fibers (stained black) from collagen fibers (stained yellow). It also labels the ground substance, staining it turquoise. Note how elastic fibers are intermixed with collagen bundles. Elastic fibers provide the elasticity needed for connective tissues to stretch and recoil to their original shape. 400x
