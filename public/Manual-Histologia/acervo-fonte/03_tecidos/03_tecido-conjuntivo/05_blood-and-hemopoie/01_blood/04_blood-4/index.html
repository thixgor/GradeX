Blood: Eosinophil

Eosinophils are granular leucocytes with heterochromatic, bilobed nuclei and bright eosinophilic granules that fill the cytoplasm. Like neutrophils, eosinophils function in tissues and readily exit the blood stream. Eosinophils play a role in allergic reactions and parasitic infections as well as phagocytosis. 1000x

Granules

The specific granules of eosinophils contain a number of enzymes and other proteins that moderate the effect of vasoactive agents in inflammation, effect the killing of helminthic parasites, and phagocytose antigen-antibody complexes.
