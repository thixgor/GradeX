Compound tubuloacinar gland

The secretory units in this gland consist of spherical acini and elongated tubules. Their secretions are immediately drained by intralobular ducts which flow into larger interlobular ducts located in the interlobular connective tissue between lobules. Interlobular ducts are often lined by a stratified columnar epithelium. Submandibular gland 400x
