Simple, coiled tubular gland

Another simple gland is the simple, coiled tubular gland. These glands consist of a very long, unbranched duct that is continuous with the coiled secretory portion. An example of this gland is this eccrine sweat gland in skin. The connection between these ducts and their glandular portions is out of the plane of section. Skin 100x
