Pseudostratified columnar epithelium

The lining epithelium of the trachea demonstrates a pseudostratified epithelium with cilia and goblet cells. Goblet cells release mucin proteins which, when hydrated, form mucus. The mucus traps inhaled particles and the entire mucous sheet is moved upwards by the beating action of the cilia. 1200x
