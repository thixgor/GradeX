Peripheral nerve: Investments

This cross section of a peripheral nerve is stained with the Masson stain to reveal its investing layers. Individual axons and their sheaths are surrounded by an endoneurium. Groups of axons form a fascicle enclosed by a perineurium. This peripheral nerve, composed of a single fascicle, retains only a thin, fragmented rim of epineurium around its periphery. 400x
