Unmyelinated axons

Unmyelinated axons are generally smaller than myelinated axons. At this magnification, groups of unmyelinated axons are just barely resolvable as small circular profiles or dots within each red outline. Each unmyelinated axon is surrounded by Schwann cell cytoplasm and plasma membrane. Unmyelinated axons conduct more slowly than do myelinated axons.
