Schwann cell

Schwann cell cytoplasm

During myelination, the axon lies in a longitudinal groove of plasma membrane indented into the Schwann cell surface. One edge of the groove wraps around the axon forming concentric membrane layers, called myelin. As membranes compact, intervening cytoplasm is eliminated. Cytoplasm is retained between membranes adjacent to the axon and at the periphery of the myelin.
