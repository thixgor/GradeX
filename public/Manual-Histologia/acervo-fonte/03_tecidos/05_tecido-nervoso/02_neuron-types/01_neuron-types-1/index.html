Multipolar neuron

This image shows a multipolar neuron in an H&E stained smear of spinal cord, a technique that displays this neuron in its entirety. Note the axon, euchromatic nucleus, nucleolus and numerous dendrites emanating from the cell body. The basophilic material in the cell body and processes is Nissl. Small nuclei are those of neuroglial cells. 200x
