Multipolar neuron

The nucleus, nucleolus and Nissl substance (RER and polysomes) are located in the cell body of a neuron. Also seen are two dendrites and an axon, the latter of which lacks RER. The space around the cell body is a common artifact of nervous tissue. Numerous glial cell nuclei are also present. 1000x
