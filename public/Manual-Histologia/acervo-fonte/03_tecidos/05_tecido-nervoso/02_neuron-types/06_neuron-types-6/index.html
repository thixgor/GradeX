Nissl substance

This electron micrograph shows a portion of a neuronal cell body with a large Nissl body. Nissl is a unique association of RER and polyribosomes which appear as discrete structures in the cytoplasm. Nissl is prominent in the cell body and dendrites but is absent from axons.
